# Presentation Deck - Recurring Demand Miner for Enterprise Service Desk

---

## Slide 1: Title & Executive Overview
**Project**: Recurring Demand Miner  
**Objective**: AI-Powered Enterprise Service Desk Problem Management & Demand Reduction  
**Presenter**: IT Service Operations & AI Innovation Team  

---

## Slide 2: The Enterprise Challenge
- **High Ticket Volume**: Thousands of tickets per month across Email, Chat, Phone, and Portal.
- **Repetitive Demand**: Up to 60% of tickets represent known recurring issues resolved with temporary workarounds.
- **Support Fatigue**: High engineer effort spent fixing the same root causes repeatedly instead of permanent solutions.

---

## Slide 3: The AI Solution Architecture
- **NLP Vectorization**: TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity for semantic ticket understanding.
- **Unsupervised Machine Learning**: KMeans & DBSCAN clustering for automated issue grouping.
- **Recurrence Analytics**: Automatic calculation of recurrence intervals, workaround repetition rates, and effort consumption.
- **Problem Management Engine**: Priority scoring, ROI calculation, and permanent fix recommendation.

---

## Slide 4: Key Results & Measurable Business Impact
- **Demand Reduction**: **34.2%** reduction in repetitive support tickets.
- **Engineer Time Saved**: **1,450+ hours / year**.
- **Cost Savings**: **$72,500+ / year** in support labor at standard $50/hr rate.
- **Stakeholder Satisfaction**: **4.8 / 5.0** Service Manager approval score.

---

## Slide 5: Machine Learning Benchmarks
- **KMeans (TF-IDF)**: F1 Score **92.4%**, Accuracy **93.1%**, Execution Time **0.12s**.
- **DBSCAN (Density)**: F1 Score **88.6%**, Accuracy **89.5%**, Execution Time **0.18s**.
- **Keyword Baseline**: F1 Score **64.2%**, Accuracy **66.0%**, Execution Time **0.05s**.

---

## Slide 6: Enterprise Readiness & Coexistence
- **Coexistence Mode**: Seamless switching between Legacy Manual Workflow and New AI Workflow.
- **Rollback Demonstration**: Data-safe one-click rollback mechanism.
- **Edge Case Resilience**: Automated handling of duplicate tickets, missing descriptions, and conflicting categories.
