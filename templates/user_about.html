What is Recurring Demand Mining?

The Enterprise Service Desk receives thousands of support requests across Email, Chat, Phone, and Web Portal. Historically, up to 60% of these tickets represent recurring issues resolved with temporary workarounds.

Our AI engine uses NLP Text Vectorization (TF-IDF) and Unsupervised Machine Learning (KMeans & DBSCAN) to group similar incidents into clusters, calculate recurrence intervals, and recommend permanent engineering fixes.
